# Marketing Consent

**Last Updated:** January 21, 2025
**Effective Date:** January 21, 2025

---

## 1. Overview

DevLog sends marketing information to provide members with better services and useful information. This consent is **optional** in accordance with Article 50 of the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection, and there is no restriction on membership registration or service use if you do not consent.

---

## 2. Types of Information

### 2.1 Marketing Information
- Introduction to new services and features
- Events, promotions, and discount benefits
- Subscription plan (Pro, Enterprise) information
- Service tips and best practices
- User surveys and feedback requests

### 2.2 Newsletter
- Official DevLog newsletter (weekly/monthly)
- Technical blog post recommendations
- Developer community news
- Trending posts and popular author introductions

---

## 3. Delivery Methods

Marketing information is sent through the following methods:

- **Email**: Email address registered at signup
- **In-service notifications**: Web/app push notifications
- **SMS/Text**: Urgent or important event information (with separate consent)

---

## 4. Frequency

- **Marketing emails**: 1-2 times per week (maximum)
- **Newsletter**: Once a week or twice a month
- **Emergency notices**: Irregular as needed

We avoid excessive sending and respect your inbox.

---

## 5. Use of Personal Information

The following personal information is used to send marketing information:

| Item | Purpose of Use |
|------|----------------|
| Email address | Send marketing information and newsletters |
| Nickname | Create personalized messages |
| Service usage history | Customized content recommendations |
| Subscription plan information | Provide plan-specific benefits |

**Retention Period**: Until consent withdrawal or membership withdrawal

---

## 6. Withdrawal Methods

You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time:

### 6.1 Change in Settings Page
1. Access [Settings page](/settings) after login
2. Turn OFF "Marketing Information" toggle in "Account Information" section
3. Changes are automatically saved

### 6.2 Unsubscribe Link in Email
- "Unsubscribe" link provided at the bottom of all marketing emails
- Immediate unsubscription with one click

### 6.3 Customer Support
- Email: support@devlog.com
- Contact form: [Support Center](/support)

**Processing Time**: Within 3 business days after unsubscribe request

---

## 7. Legal Basis

This marketing consent is based on the following laws:

- **Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection** Article 50 (Restriction on Transmission of Commercial Information)
- **Personal Information Protection Act** Article 22 (Method of Obtaining Consent)
- **Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce** Article 50-5 (Protection of Consumer Rights)

---

## 8. No Disadvantage for Non-Consent

Even if you do not consent to receive marketing information:
- ✅ Can register as a member
- ✅ Can use all services
- ✅ Can receive important notices (service operations)
- ✅ Can use core features like posting, commenting, subscriptions

However, the following benefits may not be provided:
- ❌ Event and promotion information
- ❌ Early access to new features
- ❌ Personalized content recommendations

---

## 9. Protection of Children's Personal Information

Consent to receive marketing information for children under 14 years of age requires the consent of a legal guardian. Since DevLog is only available for ages 14 and above, there is no separate legal guardian consent process.
