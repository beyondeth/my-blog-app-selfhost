# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2025

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Codebase collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the website, documentation, authentication flows, and community features.

## 2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of data:

- Account information such as email address, username, authentication provider, and consent records
- Content you submit, including posts, comments, prompts, profile text, and support requests
- Technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, approximate location derived from network metadata, and security logs
- Session and preference information such as selected language, cookie choices, and login state
- Optional marketing preferences if you explicitly opt in

## 3. How We Use Data

We use personal data to:

- Provide and secure the service
- Authenticate users and manage sessions
- Publish, display, and moderate community content
- Operate documentation, support, and consent workflows
- Investigate abuse, fraud, and platform misuse
- Improve product quality through analytics only if you consent to analytics cookies
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce platform rules

## 4. Legal Bases

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we generally rely on:

- Contract: to provide the service you requested
- Legitimate interests: to secure, operate, and improve the platform
- Consent: for optional analytics and optional marketing communications
- Legal obligation: where retention or disclosure is required by law

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies to keep the service working, including authentication, security, language preference, and session continuity.

Analytics cookies are disabled by default and are activated only after you opt in through the cookie preferences interface.

You can change your analytics cookie preference at any time from the footer cookie settings entry.

## 6. Analytics

If you opt in, Codebase may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Mixpanel in a privacy-controlled configuration. If you do not opt in, those analytics tools remain disabled.

## 7. Sharing and Processors

We may share personal data only with service providers that help us operate the platform, such as hosting, storage, authentication, email, observability, and support tooling.

We do not sell personal data.

We may disclose data if required to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or enforce our Terms of Service.

## 8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your home jurisdiction. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections and vendor commitments.

## 9. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for:

- Account operation
- Security and audit needs
- Legal compliance
- Dispute handling and abuse prevention

If you delete your account, some data may remain in backup, audit, or legal retention systems for a limited period.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete data
- Restrict or object to some processing
- Withdraw consent for optional processing
- Request data portability
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You can also manage optional marketing and analytics consent separately.

## 11. Children

The service is not intended for users below the minimum age required by applicable law. Where the service requires age confirmation, users must confirm eligibility before using the platform.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. However, no service can guarantee absolute security.

## 13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected through updated effective dates, published notice, or in-product communication where appropriate.

## 14. Contact

For privacy-related requests, use the official support channel provided on the Codebase support page.
