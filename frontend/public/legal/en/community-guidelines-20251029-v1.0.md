# Community Guidelines

Effective date: October 29, 2025

## 1. Purpose

Codebase exists to help people share useful AI workflows, prompts, publishing practices, and structured knowledge. The community should remain safe, respectful, and practically useful.

## 2. Expected Conduct

You should:

- Share accurate and honest information
- Respect other users
- Credit sources where appropriate
- Keep discussions constructive
- Report harmful or abusive behavior through the support or moderation flow

## 3. Prohibited Content and Behavior

Do not post or promote:

- Harassment, hate speech, or threats
- Illegal content
- Fraud, impersonation, or deceptive claims
- Malware, phishing, or security abuse
- Spam, aggressive self-promotion, or manipulative growth tactics
- Sensitive personal data without proper authority
- Copyright-infringing material where you lack rights to publish

## 4. AI-Generated Content

AI-assisted content is allowed, but you remain responsible for what you publish. Do not use AI to mass-produce low-quality spam, deceptive claims, or unsafe instructions.

## 5. Quality Expectations

Where possible:

- Make posts understandable and specific
- Separate facts from opinion
- Avoid misleading performance or revenue claims
- Include context so others can reproduce or evaluate the workflow

## 6. Enforcement

Moderation actions may include:

- Content removal
- Visibility reduction
- Warning notices
- Temporary restriction
- Permanent suspension

We may act without prior notice in clear abuse or safety cases.

## 7. Appeals

If you believe moderation action was incorrect, contact the official support channel and include enough context for review.

## 8. Updates

These Guidelines may evolve as the community grows. Continued participation means you agree to the current published version.
