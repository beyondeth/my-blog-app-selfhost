# Marketing Consent

Effective date: October 29, 2025

This document explains the optional consent for receiving marketing communications from Codebase.

## 1. Optional Consent

Marketing consent is optional. You can use the core service without agreeing to receive promotional messages.

## 2. What You May Receive

If you opt in, you may receive:

- Product updates
- Feature announcements
- Beta invitations
- Campaign or launch notices
- Educational content related to Codebase

## 3. How We Use This Consent

We use your opt-in status only to determine whether we may send optional marketing communications.

## 4. Withdrawal

You can withdraw marketing consent at any time through account settings, unsubscribe links, or the official support channel.

## 5. No Sale of Data

Opt-in status is not sold to third parties.
