# Newsletter Consent

Effective date: October 29, 2025

This document explains the optional consent for receiving newsletter messages from Codebase.

## 1. Optional Consent

Newsletter consent is optional and separate from required service terms.

## 2. Newsletter Content

If you opt in, newsletters may include:

- Product news
- Documentation updates
- Community highlights
- Workflow tips
- Release summaries

## 3. Frequency

Newsletter frequency may vary depending on product activity and editorial planning.

## 4. Withdrawal

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism in the email or through the support channel.

## 5. Data Use

Newsletter consent is used only to manage optional newsletter delivery and related preference records.
